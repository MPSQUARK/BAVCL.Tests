# Benchmark Disclaimer

Benchmarks in the results are not guaranteed to be representative of real-world performance. They are intended to provide a rough comparison of different implementations under controlled conditions. Actual performance may vary based on hardware, software environment, and specific use cases.

Benchmarks were taken on the following hardware and software configuration:

- **CPU**: Ryzen 9 3900X
- **GPU**: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 12GB
- **RAM**: 64GB DDR4 3600MT
- **OS**: Windows 11 Home 64-bit
